# Aura Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 24, 2026_

Aura ("the app") shows local weather and suggests activities, food and places
nearby. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

## What we collect

**We don't collect personal data.** Aura has no accounts, no analytics, no ads
and no tracking. We don't run any servers that receive your data.

## Your location

With your permission, Aura reads your device's location. The coordinates are
sent directly from your device to these services, only to get the information
the app shows:

- **Apple WeatherKit**: current weather and forecast.
- **Apple Maps**: places near you, and the place details you open.
  [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- **Open-Meteo**: backup weather, used only if WeatherKit is unavailable.
  [Open-Meteo's terms and privacy](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)

These services receive the request, including your IP address, under their own
privacy policies. Aura doesn't send them your name, contact details or any other
identifier.

You can turn location access off at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security →
Location Services → Aura**. The app can't show local weather without it.

## Apple Intelligence and Image Playground

Suggestions are written by Apple Intelligence, and images are created with
Image Playground. Both are Apple system features that run on your device or
through Apple's Private Cloud Compute, under
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Aura doesn't
receive or store what they process.

## Data stored on your device

Aura caches recent weather on your device so it loads faster
and uses less data. The Home Screen widget reads the same cache. It never leaves
your device, and deleting the app removes it.

## Children

Aura is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone,
including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
